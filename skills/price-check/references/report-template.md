# Price check: template

```markdown
## <product title> · <SKU>
<one sentence: "You are <position> of <n> at <price>; <shop> is cheapest at <price>, <x%> below you, <in stock / out of stock>.">

| | Price | Stock | Seen | vs me |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **You** | <price> | <stock> | <date> | — |
| <shop> | <price> | <stock> | <date> | <±x%> |

<chart: where every shop prices this product>

### Price history, last 90 days
<chart: my price and competitors over time>
<2–3 sentences: who led, when, how far>

### Warnings
- ⚠ Suspected wrong match: <shop> "<their title>" — <reason>. Excluded from the cheapest price above.
- <stale or out-of-stock offers, RRP violations, price basis>
```

Style: money in the channel currency with the basis named once (incl. or excl. VAT). Negative gap = the competitor is cheaper than me.
