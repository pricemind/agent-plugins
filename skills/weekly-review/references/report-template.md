# Weekly review: report template

Keep this order. Leave out a section that has no data, and say why in one line.

```markdown
## Weekly pricing review · <channel name> · <date from> – <date to>

**Headline**
- <who moved the market, in one line: "Competitors cut prices 1,240 times (2.3× last week), led by <shop> and <shop>.">
- <position, in one line: "We lost the cheapest spot on 38 products and won it on 12.">
- <the one thing to do first, in one line>

### 1. Competitor moves
<chart: cuts and increases per shop>
<2–3 sentences: who, how many, average cut, anything unusual>

### 2. Position lost and gained
<table: product · competitor · their old → new price · my price · position now (of N)>
<at most 10 rows each for lost and gained; give the totals>

### 3. Competitors out of stock
<table: competitor · my products they are out of stock on · how many are new this week (checked)>
<one line on where this is a chance to hold or raise price>

### 4. Top 10 to act on
<chart: price difference of the ten products>
| # | Product | My price | Cheapest competitor | Gap | Margin | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Caveats
- Prices are <incl./excl.> VAT (<price basis>).
- <stale or out-of-stock data, missing competitor rights, suspected mismatches>
```

Style:

- Lead with numbers, not adjectives. Use the channel currency.
- A cut is a negative percentage, an increase a positive one.
- Say "the cheapest fresh, in-stock competitor", not "the market", when you mean `lowest_competitor_price`.
